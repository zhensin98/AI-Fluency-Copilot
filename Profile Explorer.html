Organisation Overview
Luxury Goods | Fashion, Fragrance & Beauty

Chanel is a privately held French luxury fashion house founded by Gabrielle 'Coco' Chanel. The company designs, makes, and sells high-end fashion, fragrances, cosmetics, watches, and fine jewellery through more than 500 directly operated boutiques worldwide. It serves affluent clients globally, with Asia Pacific — including Singapore — as a key and growing market.

Core Service Areas
How Chanel serves stakeholders

Fashion - Ready-to-wear clothing, Haute Couture, and fashion accessories including handbags and shoes

Fragrance & Beauty - Perfumes, skincare, and makeup sold globally through boutiques and retailers

Watches & Fine Jewellery - Luxury timepieces and jewellery through dedicated boutiques worldwide

Métiers d’Art - Specialist craft workshops preserving traditional artisan skills used in Chanel collections

Retail & Client Experience - Boutique operations, client relationship programmes, and in-store luxury service standards
